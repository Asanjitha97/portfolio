Pick And Place Robot Manipulator

The objective of this project is to develop a robotic manipulator system capable of achieving a specified target while accurately detecting the correct container containing a table tennis ball. Utilizing an Arduino-based controller to control the robotic manipulator's movements and decision-making processes, the system performs precise and controlled multi-axis actions to reach desired spatial coordinates. By incorporating advanced ultrasonic sensing techniques and intelligent algorithms, the system reliably identifies the correct container with high accuracy, executing smooth, closed-loop pick-and-place manipulation.

// Peer-Reviewed Journal Publication

Effect of a Notch on the Fatigue Strength of a Gear Shaft

H. M. A. Jayasekara (Main Author) and S. D. R. Perera · Engineer: Journal of the Institution of Engineers, Sri Lanka, vol. 58, no. 2, pp. 113–118, 2025.

Geometrical discontinuities such as keyways, shoulders, and circumferential notches produce high localized stress concentrations that severely degrade shaft fatigue endurance under cyclic loading. This paper presents an exhaustive experimental and numerical investigation into stress concentration factors (\(K_t\)) and notch sensitivity (\(q\)) under alternating torsion and bending, providing accurate predictive life models for power transmission shafts.

Open Journal via DOI (10.4038/engineer.v58i2.7698)
